About Rill

Better irrigation starts with a clearer plan.

Rill is software for commercial farms, irrigation cooperatives and service teams that coordinate water across multiple zones.

Explore the productContact Rill
Our focus

Give every watering decision a record.

Irrigation plans have to make sense at the pump and in the field. Rill brings the readings, the water budget and the operator’s decision into one workspace.

Our initial market is Nigeria. We plan to expand as field evaluation supports additional crops and climates.
