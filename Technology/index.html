Forecast pipeline

From sensor readings to a decision brief.

The production forecast design separates estimation, resource allocation and explanation.

Field signals

Normalize timestamped moisture and flow readings. Flag stale or missing inputs for the operator.

Demand and constraints

Evaluate a gradient-boosted, zone-level baseline on later operating periods. Apply pump capacity, available water and zone priority.

Cited explanation

Summarize how the input and the operating limit affect the plan. Save the review brief with the source record.
