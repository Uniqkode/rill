RESPONSIBLE AI

Useful AI.
Accountable decisions.

Rill · Updated 14 November 2026. This policy describes the implementation delivered with the MVP and the boundaries of an optional configured AI service.

What the AI is for

AI forecasts crop water demand and gives irrigation teams a daily zone plan. The intended output is a water-demand forecast for each irrigation zone.

Human review

A farm operator checks sensor quality and local conditions before any watering decision. This MVP does not control pumps. The product can queue a report for review; this is not professional sign-off or an automated operational decision.

Evidence and uncertainty

Each review finding links to a source reference. The connected service checks whether text quotations occur in their cited source. An image observation is a model interpretation and must be checked by the responsible reviewer.

Data and instructions

Supplied files and text are treated as evidence. A document that asks a model to ignore the product’s instructions is not given authority to change the task.

Model and deployment status

The repository includes configurable OpenAI and Azure OpenAI adapters. A live model requires a real deployed endpoint and server credentials. The public workspace provides sample cases; their outputs are not measurements of live model performance.

Evaluation before use

Compare recommended allocations with soil trends and operator-recorded water applications across a growing period. Document error cases and review outcomes before making performance or safety claims.

Prohibited representations

Do not treat a generated report as proof of company registration, certification, customer endorsement, legal title, identity, professional approval or cloud usage. Processing estimates do not establish actual service consumption.
