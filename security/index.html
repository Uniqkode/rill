Live workspace

Controls for irrigation teams.

Workspace access

Members sign in with a confirmed email address and password. Access follows their workspace membership. Editing zones requires an owner or operator role and a paid workspace plan.

Explicit data submission

Prepared cases run locally. Connected analysis sends only entered inputs to the service address specified by the operator.

Human review

Reviewers can trace findings to a source, retain a dated report and record watering outcomes. Pump operation stays with the irrigation team.

Organizational deployment

Planned controls for production data.

ControlPlanned safeguard
Identity and permissionsAssign access by role and limit service permissions to the data needed for each operation.
Storage isolationSeparate records by organization, encrypt stored data, and validate access and retention rules before ingesting farm records.
Keys and networkRestrict access to service credentials and protect data in transit.
Operational monitoringMonitor service failures, delayed forecasts and missing field readings during pilots.
Audit retentionProtect audit records from modification and agree on a retention period with the organization.
Scope

Before connecting organizational data.

How do I sign in?

Use a confirmed email address and password. Workspace invites are tied to the recipient’s email address.

What happens to the readings I enter in the forecast?

The planner keeps readings in the current tab. You can export them with the suggested plan for your own records.

Who can manage access?

Workspace owners can invite operators and viewers. Contact your workspace owner for access changes.
