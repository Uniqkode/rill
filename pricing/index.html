Onboarding

Before you choose a plan.

What counts as a zone forecast?

One demand estimate for one irrigation zone during one scheduled update. A single update across 60 zones uses 60 forecasts.

How does annual pricing work?

The per-month equivalent is 20% lower. The full 12-month amount is billed annually under the service agreement.

Can I open a workspace for free?

Yes. Confirm your email to view the dashboard, filter zone records and export your register. A paid workspace plan unlocks adding and editing zones for owners and operators.

What is agreed during onboarding?

Inputs, access controls, model configuration, integration scope and retention are agreed before organizational use and payment.
