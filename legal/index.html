Privacy and terms of use.

Updated 19 November 2026. These terms cover the Rill website, sample workspace and optional connected analysis.

Privacy notice

Rill stores sample zone edits, review reports, workspace activity and optional project briefs in this browser. The sample session uses browser session storage. Use Reset demo data in Settings to remove Rill’s local workspace data.

When you choose connected analysis, the text and selected images you enter go to the service address you supply. Review the operator’s access and retention settings before entering actual organizational data.

The enquiry form prepares a draft. When the destination is GitHub, the issue you submit is public. When a contact email is configured, you send it through your email application. If the site loads a Calendly booking panel, Calendly handles scheduling information under its own privacy terms.

Terms of use

The public workspace is for evaluating the workflow with sample data. The responsible irrigation operator checks local conditions, input quality and pump capacity before acting on any proposed plan.

Commercial access, processing allowances, data handling and support are set in a separate service agreement. Proposed pricing describes the plan structure. A walkthrough request or a workspace session creates no payment obligation.

Availability and forecast performance depend on the configured deployment and field inputs. Download reports you intend to retain and use an appropriate system of record for operational work.
